01

Leaf Image Acquisition

The farmer or user uploads a clear top-down photograph of an affected plant leaf (Potato or Tomato) in JPG, JPEG, or PNG format.

Input: RGB Image File (Max 10 MB)
02

Image Preprocessing

Images are scaled to standard dimensions (224×224 pixels), converted to tensor representations, and normalized using standard ImageNet mean and variance channels.

Transform: Resize (224,224) + Normalize
03

DNN Deep Architecture

Employs a Deep Neural Network utilizing inverted residual bottleneck layers and depthwise separable convolutions for high efficiency and mobile execution.

Model: MobileNetV2 Transfer Learning
04

Feature Extraction

The convolutional layers isolate intricate visual indicators: concentric rings, water-soaked margins, fungal halos, and chlorotic tissue patterns.

Output: High-dimensional Feature Vector
05

Disease Classification

Fully-connected dense classifier layers calculate the highest probability match across 6 distinct disease categories (Early Blight, Late Blight, Healthy).

Loss: CrossEntropyLoss + Softmax
06

Confidence Score

Outputs a percentage confidence score (e.g. 96.8%) reflecting the model's statistical certainty based on the extracted feature characteristics.

Metric: 93.01% Validation Accuracy
07

Smart Agronomic Solution

Instantly pairs the detected disease with tailored organic fungicides, cultural pruning steps, preventive soil practices, and interactive AI voice readouts.

Output: Biological & Chemical Action Plan
